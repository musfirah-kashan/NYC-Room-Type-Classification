About

I build things end to end —
from raw data to a live product.

Full Stack Web Developer, Data Science & AI/ML enthusiast, and Software Engineering student at NED University, Class of 2029.

I'm Musfirah Kashan, a Software Engineering student who learns by building rather than just watching tutorials. I enjoy taking a project from raw data — or just an idea — all the way through to a working, deployed product, on both the web development and the data science side of the stack.

On the Data Science and ML side, I've trained and deployed several end-to-end machine learning projects: a Credit Card Fraud Detection app comparing six models and deployed live on Streamlit, a Student Exam Score Predictor, a Heart Disease Predictor built on classification models, Customer Segmentation using K-Means clustering, and exploratory data analysis on Netflix and Spotify datasets with Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn. This site's predictor — trained on NYC Airbnb listings and served through this FastAPI backend — is part of that same body of work.

On the development side, I've built role-based, production-style applications including MedSync, a healthcare management system with patient/doctor/admin portals, real-time chat, and digital prescriptions built on ASP.NET Core MVC and SQL Server; Business Nexus, a MERN-stack platform connecting entrepreneurs with investors featuring WebRTC video calls and e-signatures; and Impulse Gatherings, an event management platform.

I'm currently open to internships and full-time opportunities in Full Stack Development, Data Science, or Machine Learning. If you're hiring, collaborating, or just want to talk tech, I'd love to connect.
